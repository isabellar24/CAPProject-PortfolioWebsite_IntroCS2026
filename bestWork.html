Best Work

The Unit 4 App Project was my best work. I felt very accomplished in this project because I was able to address something more personal to me. My app, Out & About, was able to address my grandmother’s problem of loneliness. She was once our age and wanted to go “out & about” to have fun and interact, but as she got older, she was unable to do this, as she could not drive anymore and did not know what events were occurring near her. So, I made this app dedicated to her and her needs. I felt as though I checked off almost every single box that was holding her back. It made me feel very proud. I also feel that there are no apps quite like this online, so it made me feel even more accomplished to build an original piece. I also grasped the lesson very well, so the project requirements and coding came very easily to me. The onEvent blocks were definitely some of the easiest code I worked with this entire time, so I was very thankful for that.

This is my Unit 4 App Project that was made on code.org! This is the project that I consider my best work in these 2 semesters of my Intro to Computer Science class.
