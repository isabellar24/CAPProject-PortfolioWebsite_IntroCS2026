My Growth

This project below made me realize how complex code/computer science can be. It pushed me to learn more, using what I already learned to make the game. I realized that I learned so much that helped me in the long run. This project is definitely the one I struggled with the most and required the most effort. I was having difficulty figuring out why my code wasn’t working, how to switch screens, and more. It required patience and persistence to go on to different levels in my code and not give up on this game because I was struggling. I had to stay focused on what I was working on, so I wouldn’t get sidetracked and start to procrastinate. Procrastination and frustration go hand in hand, and it is no surprise that both are my enemies. This project really made me realize and learn that I had to overcome both and not let it get in the way of me finding new paths of code. After I was finished with this project, I gave myself a pat on the back for completing it. Sometimes you must go through your enemies to get to the other side. It is like a game where you must face your enemy to get to the next level, and so on. Looking back at my game and the rubric, I realized I would add a way to win the game and make edits to some of the objects. Other than that, I feel very accomplished in what I learned from our lessons and by myself.

This is my Unit 3 Project: Interactive Game that was made on code.org! I feel that it is the project that best represents my growth.
